ABOUT THE INTERPRETER

Communication is about more than words.

I earned a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from California State University, Northridge, with a minor in Childhood Development.

My academic background shapes my interpreting. Behind every conversation, there are two people trying to understand each other. I am the son of a decorated interpreter, and I grew up in a bilingual and bicultural family. I use my years of exposure to language and formal education to help bridge this gap.

As a California Court Certified Interpreter and NBCMI Certified Medical Interpreter, I provide professional Spanish-language interpreting for legal, medical, community, and professional settings.
