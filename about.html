ABOUT THE INTERPRETER

Communication is about more than words.

I earned a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from California State University, Northridge, with a minor in Childhood Development.

I’m the son of an excellent interpreter who was highly regarded in her field, so interpreting was part of my world long before it became my profession. I grew up in a bilingual and bicultural family where interpreting was part of daily life.

Behind every conversation, there are two people trying to understand each other. My background in psychology, child development, language, and culture shapes the attention I bring to context, tone, and clear communication.

As a California Court Certified Interpreter and CMI-Spanish (NBCMI), I provide English-Spanish interpreting for legal, medical, professional, community, and specialized assignments throughout Southern California.

THE WORK

Where do you need me?

My regular work includes legal and medical assignments, but interpreting has also taken me well outside the usual conference room or exam room. I’ve interpreted dubbing work, Canva Create 2026, live singing, Disneyland visits, and Los Angeles tour escort assignments.

Whatever the setting, I prepare for the assignment, review terminology in advance when appropriate, and focus on making sure the people in the room can understand each other clearly.
