ABOUT THE INTERPRETER

Communication is about more than words.

I earned a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from California State University, Northridge, with a minor in Childhood Development.

My academic background shapes my interpreting. Behind every conversation, there are two people trying to understand each other. I grew up in a bilingual and bicultural family where interpreting was part of everyday life. Together with my studies in psychology and child development, that background informs the attention I bring to context, tone, and clear communication.

As a California Court Certified Interpreter and CMI-Spanish (NBCMI), I provide English-Spanish interpreting for legal, medical, community, and professional settings throughout Southern California.

SOBRE EL INTÉRPRETE

La comunicación va más allá de las palabras.

Obtuve una licenciatura en Psicología de California State University, Northridge, con una especialización secundaria en Desarrollo Infantil.

Mi formación académica influye en mi manera de interpretar. Detrás de cada conversación hay dos personas tratando de entenderse.
